FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - ACCOUNT & BILLING

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Click "Sign Up" on our homepage and enter your email address, name, and a secure password. You will receive a verification email — click the link to activate your account. You can also sign up using your Google or Apple account.

Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your registered email address. You will receive a password reset link within 5 minutes. The link expires after 30 minutes for security purposes.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept the following payment methods:
- Credit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- Debit cards
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Shop Pay (Buy Now, Pay Later)
- Gift cards

Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes. We use 256-bit SSL encryption and are PCI DSS Level 1 compliant. We never store your full credit card number on our servers. All transactions are processed through secure payment gateways.

Q: How do I update my billing information?
A: Log into your account, go to "Account Settings" > "Payment Methods" to add, remove, or update your billing information.

Q: Can I use multiple payment methods for one order?
A: You can combine a gift card with one other payment method (credit card, PayPal, etc.). However, you cannot split an order across two credit cards.

Q: What is your Premium membership?
A: Premium membership costs $9.99/month or $89.99/year and includes:
- Free express shipping on all orders
- Early access to sales and new products
- 10% cashback on all purchases
- Priority customer support
- Exclusive member-only deals

Q: How do I cancel my Premium membership?
A: Go to "Account Settings" > "Membership" > "Cancel Membership". Your benefits will continue until the end of your current billing period. No refunds are given for partial months.

Q: I was charged twice for my order. What should I do?
A: Duplicate charges are rare but can occur. Please contact our billing team immediately with your order number. We will investigate and refund the duplicate charge within 24 hours.

Q: How do I delete my account?
A: To permanently delete your account, go to "Account Settings" > "Privacy" > "Delete Account". Please note this action is irreversible and all your order history and data will be removed after 30 days.
