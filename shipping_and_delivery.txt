FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - SHIPPING & DELIVERY

Q: What shipping options are available?
A: We offer the following shipping options:
- Standard Shipping (5-7 business days): Free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99
- Express Shipping (2-3 business days): $9.99
- Overnight Shipping (next business day): $19.99
- International Shipping (10-21 business days): Rates vary by country

Q: When will my order be shipped?
A: Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST Monday–Friday are processed and shipped the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM or on weekends are processed the next business day.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking email with a tracking number. You can use this number on our website under "Track My Order" or directly on the carrier's website.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International orders may be subject to customs duties and import taxes, which are the buyer's responsibility.

Q: What happens if my package is lost?
A: If your tracking shows no movement for more than 7 business days (domestic) or 21 business days (international), please contact our support team. We will file a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or issue a full refund.

Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?
A: Address changes can only be made within 1 hour of placing your order. Please contact support immediately with your order number and new address.

Q: Do you offer free shipping?
A: Yes! All domestic orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping. Premium members enjoy free express shipping on all orders.

Q: What carriers do you use?
A: We primarily use UPS, FedEx, and USPS. The carrier is selected based on your location and the shipping method chosen at checkout.
